ClickUp Canny — Custom Fields signal extraction

Public-feedback corpus analysis · feedback.clickup.com · prepared 2026-04-19

1. TL;DR

Lens: signals that constrain or shape AI Custom Fields. Broader CF posts are kept only when they materially affect AI CF behaviour (dashboard ingestion of AI outputs, rollup composition of AI summaries, Add-existing for AI CFs, etc.).

  • Juliana Stanisauskiene's January 21 2026 comment on the AI Custom Fields thread is the canonical statement of the most-cited AI CF gap: AI CFs are text-only, so AI-derived numeric values can't be summed or charted. ClickUp has not replied. Full text in §7.
  • AI billing per user is the largest AI-tagged ask in the corpus by ~5× — 842 votes, marked "not on the roadmap" March 19 2026. ClickUp's usage-based-billing counter-proposal was rejected by the comment thread; admins want per-seat governance. Direct risk to AI CF adoption since AI CFs are credit-consuming by design.
  • "AI on a non-text-non-dropdown type" recurs across 8+ posts — Rating, Number, Currency, Percent, Linked Record, Doc, Duration, Time. These are roadmap-distinct from prompt-tuning asks. Today only Long Text and Dropdown are AI-capable.
  • The seam between AI CFs and the broader CF graph is the hot zone. AI Agents can't read custom attachment fields (56v, planned); attachments-to-CF extraction (120v, planned) is in flight. Caroline Ginty has confirmed AI CFs will support attachment reads.
  • AI CF outputs are dashboard-blind. Formula fields in Dashboard (2,700v, partial) and Subtask Rollup (2,000v, "not on committed roadmap" 6 yrs) are the structural reasons AI CF data can't aggregate or report — even though those posts pre-date AI CFs.
  • The new user AI CF first experience is the unconfigured Free plan. Free plan caps CFs at 60 "uses" (every change counts), which 238 voters hit before they understand what 'use' means. AI CFs amplify the same pattern — each AI run is also a credit. Education and cost transparency need pairing.
  • Corpus context. 121 distinct posts after duplicate-merging across Feature Requests, Public API, ClickUp Mobile, AI Super Agents, and Changelog. 20 are AI-tagged; 8 ask for AI on a new underlying type.

2. Methodology

Two-pass crawl of feedback.clickup.com. Pass 1: ~30 Google site-restricted queries surfaced the high-relevance post URLs. Pass 2: direct browser fetches captured real vote counts, official status badges, dates, and body text for the top 25 priority posts. Long-tail posts retain a documented prominence proxy (queries × 10).

  • 121 unique posts after dropping 2 merge-duplicates; 25 with confirmed vote counts.
  • Sample is keyword-driven (Canny exposes no full board listing). Likely under-counts long-tail and very recent submissions.
  • Bolded vote counts in the appendix are confirmed; greyed numbers are proxy.

3. Volume & momentum

The AI cluster is dominated by AI billing per user (842v, not on roadmap) — ~5× any other AI-tagged ask. The next tier is a thin one: AI Custom Fields anchor (185v, complete), Extract-from-attachments (120v, planned), Custom Prompts in AI (99v, complete via Saved Prompts). Beneath that, every AI ask is <60 votes. The AI conversation in the corpus is more about cost control and graph-completeness than feature variety.

The overall top is shown for context. These pre-date AI CFs but shape the substrate they run on: dashboard ingestion (Formula in Dashboard, 2,700v), aggregation (Subtask Rollup, 2,000v), formula composability (Use-any-field, 1,500v), permissions (2,200v, shipped). Custom Field Manager (3,500v, shipped) is where AI CF management lives.

By year of first post, AI volume picks up sharply from 2024 onward as Brain rolled out; non-AI CF posts cluster in 2020–2023 (the 3.0 / CF Manager era).

4. Theme breakdown

Post-count vs vote-weight flips three rankings. Dashboard/aggregation is 5th by count but 1st by votes (4,910 — Formula-in-Dashboard and Subtask Rollup). Bulk management climbs to 3rd by votes on Custom Field Manager's 3,500. Permissions/sharing punches above its 6-post count via Granular Permissions. AI field gap is 5th by count but 8th by votes — the AI cluster is broad but each individual ask is small (1–120 votes). Theme-by-vote is the better priority signal: dashboard/aggregation/rollup carries the most accumulated weight, and most of it is Open.

5. Status breakdown

65% Open, 7% In Progress, 27% Complete. Complete is concentrated in 3.0-era infrastructure (CF Manager, Granular Permissions, Convert, Reorder, Searchable, Bulk Actions) plus the AI CF launch. Open splits two ways: cross-cutting power-user gaps (Subtask Rollup, Use-any-field-in-formulas, Conditional CFs) and the AI extension cluster (AI on Number/Rating, AI on attachments, per-user AI billing).

6. Strategic gaps surfaced

Posts where the request points to a deeper product gap, not just a missing feature. Selected to bias toward the parent project's two goals — new user adoption of AI CFs, and power-user CSAT for AI CFs. Each pick is annotated with its AI CF connection: why this constrains or shapes AI Custom Fields, even when the post itself isn't AI-tagged. Direct-AI picks are listed first.

DIRECT-AIAI Custom Fields (anchor) — and Juliana Stanisauskiene's Jan 2026 comment · 185 votes · Complete

"AI Custom Fields appear to be text-only. There's no option to set Number/Currency."

Post shipped Feb 2025; the active conversation moved into the comments. Juliana's Jan 21 2026 ask is unanswered.

AI CF connection: Today AI CFs are pre-configured Long Text + Dropdown. Number/Currency/Percent require a NEW underlying type, not a settings change. Prompt-UX and new-type asks belong on separate roadmap tracks.

DIRECT-AIAI billing per user · 842 votes · Not on the roadmap

"Charging per number of total seats makes this feature unjustifiable."

Largest AI-tagged post in the corpus. ClickUp's Mar 19 2026 usage-based-billing counter-proposal was rejected by the comment thread.

AI CF connection: AI CFs are credit-consuming by design. Without per-user governance, admins block AI entry-points entirely. Direct risk to both adoption (new users) and CSAT (power users).

DIRECT-AIClickUp AI — Extract data from attachments · 120 votes · Planned

"ClickUp AI extract each data from attached word form to each custom field."

Caroline Ginty Jul 2 2025: 'confirmed that we plan to support this with AI Custom Fields as well.'

AI CF connection: Attachment reads (PDF/Word/image) unlock recruitment, invoice processing, and compliance — high-leverage AI CF use cases blocked today.

DIRECT-AIEnable AI Agents to Access Custom Attachment Fields · 56 votes · Planned

"Build precise, field-specific workflows."

Sister post to Extract-from-attachments. Stephen Carlos' use case: per-field-type AI logic for resumes, background checks, IDs.

AI CF connection: Custom attachment FIELDS specifically. Closing this seam moves AI CFs from leaf-node to composable.

DIRECT-AIAI Filled Rating Field (and the 'AI on numeric type' pattern) · 1 vote · Future

"AI to populate the Rating field."

Single-vote canary, but joins a recurring pattern: 8+ posts ask for AI on Rating, Number, Currency, Percent, Linked Record, Doc, Duration, or Time.

AI CF connection: Defines the strategic decision: stay on Long Text + Dropdown, or invest in new AI-capable underlying types. The latter is the answer to Juliana.

AI-CONSTRAININGFormula fields in Dashboard · 2,700 votes · Planned

"Unsupported data type in formula field."

Partial support shipped. Users still blocked on TODAY()-based formulas, formula-of-formulas, aggregated calculations.

AI CF connection: AI CF outputs (text by default) can't render in Dashboard widgets. Same ingestion gap as formulas. Fix once and AI-CF data flows for free — exactly what Juliana asked for.

AI-CONSTRAININGRollup Custom Field values from subtasks to parent task · 2,000 votes · Future

"On our radar, but not on the committed roadmap."

Open since January 2020. Caroline Ginty Nov 2025: still 'on radar, not committed' — a 6-year standoff.

AI CF connection: Without subtask rollup, an AI Summary on a parent can't compose child summaries; AI Sentiment can't aggregate to portfolio. AI CFs become leaf-only.

AI-CONSTRAININGUse any field in formulas (dropdowns, text, checkbox) · 1,500 votes · Future

"Only possible to reference numeric or date fields in formulas."

Notion/Airtable parity gap. Formula refs constrained to Number/Date.

AI CF connection: AI Categorize / T-shirt Size output dropdowns; AI Summary outputs text. None can be referenced in a Formula CF, so AI classifications can't drive computed metrics.

AI-CONSTRAININGAllow adding formula CFs to multiple locations ('Use field from library') · 79 votes · Open

"Formula CFs don't appear in 'Use field from library'."

Direct evidence the Add-existing surface has type-class blind spots.

AI CF connection: Same architectural risk applies to AI CFs. Verify AI CF variants appear in 'Use field from library' before shipping the Add-existing redesign.

AI-CONSTRAININGCustom Fields in Free Plan (60-use cap) · 238 votes · Open since 2019

"Every change counts as a 'use.'"

Free plan caps CFs at 60 'uses' (every change counts). Multiple comments report churn to Notion.

AI CF connection: The new user AI CF first experience IS this Free plan. Each AI run is also a credit; AI CFs stack two billing surprises in the same flow. Education + cost transparency need pairing.

7. Watchlist confirmations

Juliana Stanisauskiene — Number/Currency AI fields + Dashboard aggregation (Jan 2026) — CONFIRMED

Located on direct browse of the AI Custom Fields thread. Format: the referenced item is a comment on that thread, not a standalone post — dated January 21, 2026. The host post itself has 185 votes and is marked completed (Jack Dowd, Feb 13 2025). Juliana's comment opens with: "We've noticed a limitation with AI Custom Fields and wanted to ask about your product roadmap." She lays out three asks for the ClickUp team:

  • Make Number/Currency (Money) a valid AI Custom Field type — today AI CFs are text-only, so AI-derived numeric values are stored as strings.
  • Provide an automatic conversion/casting feature, OR clarify whether the text-only behaviour is intentional.
  • Make AI CF values usable for Dashboard aggregations (charts, sums) — today they aren't, since Dashboards expect true numeric/currency fields.

The closing question — "If there's a recommended workaround … to make AI outputs dashboard-friendly today, we'd really appreciate your suggestion" — is unanswered as of this report's date. ClickUp has not replied to the comment publicly.

Note: she is also a confirmed voter on the AI Custom Fields post (top of voters list) and on Formula Column Calculations.

AI billing surprise / credit consumption — CONFIRMED

Anchor: AI billing per user (842v, "not on the roadmap" Mar 19 2026 with usage-based-billing counter-proposal). Comment thread shows the response was unconvincing — admins want governance and per-seat control, not metered usage. Adjacent: Super Agent Credit allowance (9v, completed — 75% discount on first 3 agent runs, avg cost shown), Hide AI buttons if not purchased (25v, complete), Allow Individual Users to Disable AI (8v, open).

"Add existing" tab behaviour — INDIRECTLY CONFIRMED

Allow adding "formula" custom field type to multiple locations (79v) is direct evidence the Add-existing / Use-field-from-library surface has type-class blind spots — formula CFs are excluded. Same risk class for AI CFs.

Custom Field Manager bulk operations — CONFIRMED

Custom Field Manager (3,500v, complete). Comments flag two regressions from 2.0 (lost manual ordering, lost pinning) and the Business-Plus gate on move-field. Adjacent: Bulk custom field removal (4v, open), Convert Custom Fields (575v, complete; conversion is one-way).

Mobile field creation — CONFIRMED OPEN

Add & Edit Custom Fields in Mobile, plus Mobile Task creation — Custom Fields (in progress) and Signature CF on mobile.

Progress field manual vs. auto — CONFIRMED

Convert Progress (Manual) into Progress (Auto), Auto-Progress driven by CFs, Auto-Progress nested subtasks, Weighted/formula auto-progress.
